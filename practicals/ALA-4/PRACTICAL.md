## 1. Case Study Aim & Objectives
* Evaluate published contractual SLAs for compute, storage, and database services.
* Calculate permitted downtime across availability tiers (99.0% to 99.999%).
* Contrast financial service credit schedules and understand provider exclusion boundaries.
* Construct a comprehensive 5x5 Likelihood x Consequence operational risk matrix.
* Map multi-cloud architectures to compliance standards (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, GDPR).

## 2. Permitted Monthly Downtime (30-day billing cycle = 43,200 mins)
* **99.0% ("Two Nines"):** 432.0 minutes (7.2 hours)
* **99.9% ("Three Nines"):** 43.2 minutes
* **99.95%:** 21.6 minutes
* **99.99% ("Four Nines"):** 4.32 minutes
* **99.999% ("Five Nines"):** 25.9 seconds
